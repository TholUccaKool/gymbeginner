FitTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

FitTrack is a fitness and nutrition tracking app that stores your data locally on your device. This policy describes exactly what data the app collects, where it goes, and what stays private.

1. Data stored on your device

All of the following data is stored in your browser's localStorage (or the app's local storage on iOS/Android via Capacitor). It never leaves your device except as described in Section 2.

CategoryWhat's stored
ProfileExperience level, training days, preferred workout days
Body metrics (coach users only)Age, height (cm), weight (kg), fitness goal, physique preference, equipment access, injuries, diet preference
NutritionCalorie and protein targets; meal log entries (name, calories, protein, date)
WorkoutsWorkout history (exercises, sets, reps, weight, completion status, duration, notes)
Coaching stateWorkout and nutrition streaks, missed workout log, weekly review summaries
Exercise progressionLast weight and reps per exercise, suggested next weight
PreferencesNotification settings, premium status (demo only), exercise image cache

There are no user accounts, no login, no email addresses, and no passwords. You are not identified by name or contact information anywhere in the app.

2. Data that leaves your device

The only feature that transmits data off your device is the AI Coach chat. When you send a message to the coach, the following context is sent alongside your message to a server-side function:

  • Your chat message text
  • Today's calorie and protein targets
  • Today's logged meals (name, calories, protein)
  • Today's calorie/protein totals
  • Your training schedule (which days, how many per week)
  • Today's planned workout type and completion status
  • Behavioral summary: current workout streak, nutrition streak, missed workouts in the last 14 days
  • Current date and day of week

This data is sent to a Supabase Edge Function (a server-side proxy), which forwards it to Lovable's AI gateway (ai.gateway.lovable.dev), which routes to Google's Gemini model for processing. The Edge Function is stateless — it does not store your data. The response is returned to your device and that is the end of the transaction.

Your body metrics (age, height, weight), injury information, and diet preferences are not sent to the AI coach. Only the context listed above is transmitted.

3. Data that is never collected

  • No email, name, phone number, or account credentials
  • No location or GPS data
  • No camera, microphone, or contacts access
  • No advertising identifiers or device fingerprinting
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or error-reporting services (no Google Analytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, or similar)
  • No cookies (the app uses localStorage only)

4. Third-party services

ServicePurposeData sent

GitHub (yuhonas/free-exercise-db) Exercise demonstration images None — the app fetches static image files; no user data is sent

No other third-party services receive data from the app.

5. Payment and subscription

The premium/pro features shown in the app are a demo. No real payment processing exists. No payment information is collected, stored, or transmitted. The "purchase" button sets a local flag in your browser — nothing more.

6. Notifications

FitTrack can send local notifications (workout reminders, meal tracking reminders) using your device's built-in notification system. These are scheduled and triggered entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

7. Data retention and deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • To delete all data: Clear your browser's site data for FitTrack, or uninstall the app.
  • There is no server-side data to delete. The AI Coach Edge Function is stateless and does not retain your messages or context after responding.
  • Coach memory (streaks, nutrition days) is automatically trimmed to the last 30 days. Weekly reviews are trimmed to the last 12 weeks.

8. Children

FitTrack does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app does not collect identifying information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example, if cloud sync, user accounts, or real payment processing are added), this policy will be updated and the "last updated" date revised accordingly.

10. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository or contact the developer directly.
